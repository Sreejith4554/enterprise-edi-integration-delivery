# Release checklist

- [ ] Candidate commit recorded and diff reviewed
- [ ] No secrets or real company/customer data included
- [ ] Lint and isolated tests pass on candidate
- [ ] Real Compose build, health and smoke pass on candidate
- [ ] Both formats, duplicates, invalid input, correction and ERP recovery verified
- [ ] Schema compatibility and backup/restore reviewed
- [ ] Open defects and known limitations reviewed
- [ ] Go/no-go decision recorded
- [ ] Rollback and outstanding-message reconciliation understood
- [ ] Post-release sample orders and acknowledgement checks complete
- [ ] Owner UAT, operations handover and demonstration complete
